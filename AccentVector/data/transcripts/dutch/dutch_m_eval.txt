The north wind and the sun were disputing which was stronger. 
They agreed that one who first succeeded in making the traveller take his cloak off should be considered stronger than the other.
Then the north wind blew as hard as he could, but the more he blew the more closely did the traveller fold his cloak around him.
And at last the north wind gave up the attempt.
Then the sun shone out warmly and immediately the traveller took off his cloak.
The sun was stronger of the two.
